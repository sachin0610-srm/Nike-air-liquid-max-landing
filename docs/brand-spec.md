# Nike Air Liquid Max Brand Spec

## Source Notes

- Product: Nike Air Liquid Max.
- Official source: Nike Newsroom release, "Nike Sportswear Introduces the Nike Air Liquid Max", published March 10, 2026.
- Asset source: Nike Newsroom media images from the same release.
- Product facts used: point-loaded Nike Air, low-profile upper, three-layer textured printing, green launch colorway, chrome Swoosh, translucent outsole, March 26 availability through SNKRS and select Nike retail partners.

## Visual Direction

- Mood: bold, kinetic, close to the floor, high contrast.
- Palette: black, white, volt green, pale ice, chrome gray, and a small flash of warm signal yellow.
- Typography: system sans stack with condensed, oversized display scale.
- Motion: scroll-telling sequence that makes the product feel suspended, then reveals upper, air geometry, and launch CTA.
